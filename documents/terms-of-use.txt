2. WHAT THE APP DOES

LuckyCat is a touchscreen entertainment game for domestic cats under adult supervision and a playful number-selection tool for their adult owners. The owner selects the number of tickets, numbers per ticket and number range. During the hunt, touches on the mouse produce numbers that form the displayed combinations, called “tickets”. Favorites stores game configurations for reuse.
