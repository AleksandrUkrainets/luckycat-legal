LUCKYCAT PRIVACY POLICY

Version: 2.0
Effective date: 9 November 2026

Policy page: LuckyCat Privacy Policy (https://aleksandrukrainets.github.io/luckycat-legal/privacy-policy/)

1. RESPONSIBLE PERSON AND SCOPE

Oleksandr Ukrainets, an individual residing in Poland without a registered company, is the Developer of LuckyCat and the controller for the support processing described here. For all questions, including privacy, rights requests and complaints, contact lucky.cat.support@gmail.com.

This Policy covers the LuckyCat Android application, package com.lucky.cat.ou, voluntary support correspondence, and visits to its public legal pages. The first distribution markets are the 27 EU Member States and the United States. The App is intended for adults aged 18 or older, or the higher age of legal majority where they reside, supervising their cats.

The GDPR applies to personal-data processing in the context of the Developer's establishment in Poland, including relevant support processing for people in the United States. Additional applicable US rights remain available. Nothing here asserts a blanket exemption from privacy laws because the App is free or the Developer is an individual.

2. CURRENT APP DATA PRACTICES

The current release is free, with no advertising, purchases, subscriptions, developer-operated backend, user accounts or cloud-save service. The App does not ask for your name, postal address, telephone number, payment details or login credentials, and does not automatically send gameplay records to the Developer.

Unity Analytics, Engine Diagnostics, Cloud Diagnostics and other app telemetry services are disabled for this release. No advertising SDK or advertising-ID access is used. The App does not track people across other apps or websites.

Generative AI tools helped prepare some development artwork. The App has no runtime AI service and does not submit user or gameplay data to AI models. It does not perform profiling or automated individual decision-making producing legal or similarly significant effects within Article 22 GDPR.

This description of the App is distinct from Google Play's platform processing, operating-system backups, web hosting and information you voluntarily email. Those activities are explained below.

3. LOCAL RECORDS AND OPERATING-SYSTEM BACKUPS

The App uses local storage for the following purposes:

Language and game configuration — Remember selected settings, including ticket count, numbers per ticket and number range; until changed, overwritten or local app data is removed

Favorite game configurations — Reuse settings explicitly saved to Favorites; until removed through Favorites or local app data is removed. These are settings, not an archive of caught-number tickets

Last completed series — Retain the latest completed numbers, replay configuration and whether Results should reopen. A later completed series replaces the main record. A recovery copy can retain an earlier record until it too is overwritten or removed

Terms acceptance — Store the accepted Terms version and UTC acceptance time; until updated or local app data is removed

Required update version — Store the highest applicable newer version reported available through Google Play. LuckyCat uses this for its own new-series restriction; the requirement is cleared when the installed version reaches that code or a higher code, subject to the limitations in the Terms

Early-user ad-free entitlement — An eligible pre-advertising release stores a free local flag after successful saving. It has no scheduled expiry and will be honoured by a later advertising release while available to the installation


The last-result files include a backup used for recovery; clearing the Results restoration marker does not itself promise erasure of every saved copy. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes the local app copy, subject to system backup/restore behaviour. These actions may also remove the early-user entitlement and acceptance record. There is no developer account from which to restore them. The Developer cannot remotely inspect or delete local game files. This does not affect your rights concerning data held in support correspondence.

These records are not automatically sent to the Developer. You may nevertheless choose to send a screenshot or other record to support. The operating system may back up or restore app preferences and files, including update and entitlement flags, according to Android's backup rules, the App's configuration and your device settings. Such copies may be held in your Google account or transferred during device migration. The Developer has no access to your personal OS backups; recovery and portability are not guaranteed. See Android Auto Backup (https://developer.android.com/identity/data/autobackup) and Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Local storage is not automatically exempt from ePrivacy rules just because it contains no name or advertising ID. Where storage or access is strictly necessary for a service you requested, applicable ePrivacy rules permit it without separate consent. Accepting Terms does not provide consent for non-essential storage. Any future non-essential storage requiring consent must wait for that separate consent.

4. VOLUNTARY EMAIL SUPPORT

When you email lucky.cat.support@gmail.com, the Developer receives your email address, sender display name, message, any attachments you choose to include and ordinary email routing metadata. Contacting support is optional and not required to play. If information needed to understand or respond to a request is missing, it may not be possible to resolve it.

Please provide only information needed for the request. Do not send identity documents, payment credentials, precise location, health information or other sensitive personal information, or unnecessary information about children or other people. The App does not request or automatically collect those categories. Free-text correspondence may nevertheless contain them. Unneeded information received in this way is deleted; any exceptional retention or further use must have a specific lawful basis and, for special-category data, meet the additional applicable conditions. It is not used for advertising or profiling.

A. Purposes and legal bases

- General support and technical or safety enquiries: the legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us and maintaining the operation and safety of LuckyCat, balanced against their rights — Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.
- Objectively necessary contractual support: performance of the agreement or relevant steps requested by the individual — Article 6(1)(b).
- A specific EU or Polish legal obligation: for example, handling a data-rights request — Article 6(1)(c), where that obligation applies.
- Establishing, exercising or defending a concrete legal claim: necessary records under Article 6(1)(f); Article 17(3)(e) may provide an exception to erasure, rather than being a separate Article 6 legal basis.

We do not treat sending an email as consent to unrelated processing. Ordinary support records are not kept indefinitely in case a hypothetical dispute arises.

B. Retention and deletion

Ordinary support correspondence is kept for no more than 12 months after the enquiry is closed. Closure is recorded when the enquiry has been resolved or there is no further support action to take; a follow-up requiring action reopens it. Applicable requests for earlier erasure are considered under section 8.

Deletion covers the relevant correspondence and attachments under the Developer's control, including incoming and sent messages, archived/All Mail messages and Trash. Records are reviewed and deleted in time to meet the stated period. This is deletion of the relevant records, not of the entire mailbox.

If a specific legal obligation or concrete claim requires or justifies longer retention, only necessary records are kept, their use is restricted, and the need is reviewed. They are deleted when that necessity ends, subject to applicable legal duties. Resolution of a dispute does not automatically end every applicable retention duty. Google's own operational logs or residual backups follow its retention processes, which the Developer does not independently control.

C. Access and handling

On the Developer's side, only the Developer has access to the support mailbox. There is no automatic forwarding or external archive of support messages. Appropriate account and device security measures must be maintained to protect correspondence; this Policy does not claim a particular security certification or that email is end-to-end encrypted. Before disclosing correspondence in response to a rights request, the requester and the scope of disclosure are checked proportionately to protect other people's data.

7. CHILDREN, TEENAGERS AND AGE INFORMATION

The intended human audience is adults aged 18 or older, or the higher local age of legal majority. The App does not target children or teenagers below that threshold or request their personal information. The current build described here has no technical age-verification mechanism and receives no platform age signals. A store rating or statement in Terms is not proof of age verification or compliance with applicable age-assurance laws. Any required mechanism and its disclosures must be in place before the distribution or processing for which the law requires them.

If support learns that a message is from a person below the applicable age threshold, it is not used for marketing or profiling. Processing is limited to lawful, necessary handling of the enquiry, a rights request, a parent/guardian contact, a safety issue or deletion. Unneeded records are deleted promptly. Any necessary exception must meet the applicable child-data and other legal requirements, including COPPA where it applies; it does not authorize unrestricted retention of minor correspondence.

Introducing age signals would require a truthful update of this Policy before that processing starts, specifying the data, purpose, recipients, retention and legal basis. No such integration is represented as already operating.

8. DATA RIGHTS AND RESPONSE TIMES

You may request access to and correction or erasure of personal data held in support, restriction of processing where the law permits, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Restriction is distinct from objection and can apply, for example, while disputed accuracy is checked. Erasure is subject to the applicable legal exceptions, including necessary legal obligations or claims; it is not limited to a mandatory “Legal Hold”.

Portability applies to data you provided where processing is automated and based on consent or contract. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. These rights apply with their statutory conditions; we do not require you to accept advertising to exercise them.

Write to lucky.cat.support@gmail.com. Using the address from your original enquiry normally helps verification. If that is unavailable, or an authorized representative acts for you, contact us to arrange a proportionate alternative. We do not routinely request identity documents or create identifiers solely to locate inaccessible local game files. We may request only the additional information reasonably necessary to establish identity or authority.

We respond without undue delay and within one calendar month, or sooner if applicable law requires. A lawful extension is used only when its conditions are met. Under GDPR this can be up to two additional months, with reasons notified within the first month; any shorter applicable limit takes precedence. If we decline a request, we explain the reason and available remedies. Requests are normally free; any lawful exception for manifestly unfounded or excessive requests must be explained rather than applied automatically.

You may complain to Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych (PUODO), ul. Stanisława Moniuszki 1A, 00-014 Warszawa, Poland — uodo.gov.pl (https://uodo.gov.pl) — or another competent supervisory authority, including in your EU habitual residence, workplace or place of the alleged infringement. Access to judicial remedies is preserved.
