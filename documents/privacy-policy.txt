2. THE GAME DOES NOT COLLECT OR TRANSMIT GAMEPLAY DATA

The current version of the Game is free and operates offline. It does not provide user accounts, advertising, in-app purchases, analytics, tracking, profiling, cloud features, social features, or developer-operated servers.

The Game does not collect or transmit gameplay information, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, audio, health information, payment information, or other personal data to the developer. The developer does not sell gameplay data or share gameplay data with third parties.

Unity Engine Diagnostics, Unity Analytics, Cloud Diagnostics, advertising services, and other telemetry services are disabled for this release.

3. INFORMATION STORED ONLY ON YOUR DEVICE

The Game stores a small amount of information locally on your device so that it can remember your choices:

- language preference;
- light or dark display theme;
- favourite game configurations and selected gameplay settings;
- the version of the Terms of Use that was accepted; and
- the UTC date and time at which that version of the Terms of Use was accepted.

This information remains on your device. It is not transmitted to the developer or used to identify, track, or profile you. It remains until it is overwritten by a later choice, removed through the Game where that option exists, cleared through your device's application settings, or deleted when you uninstall the Game. Clearing the Game's data or uninstalling the Game also removes the locally stored Terms acceptance record, so the Game may ask for acceptance again.

4. VOLUNTARY SUPPORT COMMUNICATIONS

If you contact lucky.cat.support@gmail.com, you voluntarily provide the email address from which you write, the content of your message, and any attachments or other information that you choose to send.

The developer uses support communications only to:

- respond to and manage your request;
- investigate technical, safety, abuse, or security issues;
- keep a reasonable record of the support provided; and
- establish, exercise, or defend legal claims or comply with a legal obligation when necessary.

Depending on the circumstances, the legal basis under the General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") is taking steps at your request, the developer's legitimate interests in operating and protecting the Game and responding to users, compliance with a legal obligation, or the establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims.

Please do not send information that is unnecessary for your request, particularly sensitive information or personal data about children or other people.

Support email is provided through Gmail, a service of Google. Google may process technical and account information required to deliver and secure email under its own terms and privacy notice. You can review Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Support correspondence is normally deleted no later than 12 months after the request is closed. A relevant record may be kept longer when reasonably necessary to comply with law, resolve an active dispute, prevent abuse, or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. It will then be deleted when that reason no longer applies, subject to mandatory retention requirements.

5. PUBLIC LEGAL PAGES

The public Privacy Policy and Terms of Use are hosted with GitHub Pages. The developer does not place analytics, advertising, cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party scripts on those pages.

GitHub may automatically process limited technical request data, such as an IP address, browser or device information, and request logs, to deliver and protect GitHub Pages. The developer does not receive a visitor-level analytics feed from these pages. GitHub's processing is governed by its own privacy notice, available at https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

6. SHARING, SERVICE PROVIDERS, AND INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

Because gameplay and local preference data are not transmitted to the developer, the developer does not disclose that data to anyone.

For the limited services described above, Google provides email infrastructure and GitHub provides public-page hosting. Those providers may process information in countries outside the European Economic Area. Their privacy notices describe their roles, safeguards, and available transfer mechanisms. Personal data may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect legal rights, safety, or the security of the Game or support channel.

The developer does not sell personal data and does not share personal data for cross-context behavioural advertising.

7. CHILDREN AND ANIMAL USERS

The Game and its support channel are intended for adult pet owners who are at least 18 years old. The Game is designed to be used with an animal only under an adult's supervision; an animal is not a contracting user.

The Game does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If the developer learns that a child has sent personal data through the support channel without appropriate involvement of a parent or guardian, the developer will delete it as soon as reasonably practicable unless retention is required by law or needed to protect the child or another person. A parent or guardian may request deletion by emailing lucky.cat.support@gmail.com.

8. YOUR DATA PROTECTION RIGHTS

If the GDPR or similar law applies to information held by the developer, you may have the right to request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability, or to object to processing. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing that occurred before withdrawal. Some rights depend on the circumstances and may be limited by applicable law, the rights of other people, or the need to preserve evidence for a legal claim.

The Game's local information is not accessible to the developer. You can control or delete it directly by changing Game or device settings, clearing application data, or uninstalling the Game.

To exercise a right concerning support correspondence, email lucky.cat.support@gmail.com. The developer may request information reasonably necessary to verify the request and will respond within the period required by applicable law.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority. In Poland, this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, UODO): https://uodo.gov.pl/en.

9. AUTOMATED DECISIONS, PROFILING, AND DATA PROTECTION OFFICER

The developer does not use personal data for automated decision-making or profiling. The developer has not appointed a Data Protection Officer because one is not required for the processing described in this Policy. Privacy questions may be sent directly to lucky.cat.support@gmail.com.

10. SECURITY

The developer uses reasonable organisational and technical measures appropriate to the limited support information handled. However, no device, email service, hosting service, or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should keep your device protected and avoid sending unnecessary personal or sensitive information by email.

11. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If the Game's data practices change, the developer will review those practices before release and, where required, update this Policy, the Google Play Data safety declaration, and any consent or opt-out mechanism.

A new Policy version and effective date will be published in the Game and on the public legal pages before or together with the Game update to which it applies. Material changes will be presented with appropriate notice. Earlier versions may be retained for reference. Changes will not reduce rights that have already arisen under mandatory law.
